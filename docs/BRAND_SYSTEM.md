# GnegnoliOS Brand System

## Identity

GnegnoliOS should communicate precision, quality, solidity, reliability, and prestige. Every interface and asset should feel minimal, dark, industrial, elegant, and enterprise-grade.

The visual system is built around:

- Matte black surfaces
- Charcoal hierarchy
- Forged steel edges
- Subtle dark crimson accents
- High contrast typography
- Cathedral-inspired symmetry
- Quiet material texture

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matte Black | `#0D0D0D` | Primary background |
| Charcoal | `#1B1B1B` | Raised surfaces |
| Steel | `#323232` | Dividers and secondary panels |
| Graphite | `#4D4D4D` | Muted controls and inactive states |
| Dark Crimson | `#7A0F16` | Accent, focus, progress, selection |
| Metal Silver | `#A8A8A8` | Secondary text and strokes |
| White | `#EDEDED` | Primary text |

Avoid bright blue, fluorescent green, neon purple, rainbows, and saturated gamer palettes.

## Typography

- Display: Cinzel or Cormorant Garamond
- Interface/body: Inter or IBM Plex Sans
- Terminal/code: JetBrains Mono

Use large typography only where the surface deserves ceremony: first boot, installer hero views, website hero sections, and major product identity screens. Operational UI should be dense, calm, and scannable.

## Logo Rules

The logo must:

- Contain no text
- Work at 24x24 pixels
- Be flat, symmetric, sharp, and vector-first
- Read as premium, forged, heraldic, and corporate
- Work on black, silver, and single-color contexts

Acceptable references include runic geometry, shields, arches, cathedral windows, swords, monoliths, sacred geometry, and restrained heraldry.

Do not use skulls, demons, wings, dragons, monsters, anime styling, or decorative excess.

## UI Rules

- Prefer matte black and charcoal surfaces with realistic contrast.
- Use subtle shadows and small radius values.
- Use crimson sparingly for intent, focus, danger, progress, and active states.
- Keep spacing generous on presentation surfaces and compact in operational tools.
- Avoid RGB lighting, neon glows, and ornamental fantasy decoration.
- Make every control feel like serious software, not a game skin.

## Wallpaper Rules

Official wallpapers should be dark, architectural, atmospheric, and material-driven. Use cathedral scale, fog, stone, steel, volcanic surfaces, and restrained embers. Do not include characters, people, monsters, weapons as the main subject, or literal horror imagery.

## Creative Prompt

Design assets for GnegnoliOS, a premium Arch Linux distribution with a luxury enterprise identity. Combine corporate minimalism, gothic architecture, dark fantasy atmosphere, forged steel, volcanic stone, brushed metal, cathedral-inspired geometry, medieval heraldry, industrial luxury, elegant symmetry, matte black surfaces, subtle crimson accents, cinematic chiaroscuro lighting, realistic materials, premium UI/UX aesthetics, Apple-level polish, IBM professionalism, Fractal Design minimalism, and high-end software branding. Avoid horror, demons, skulls, neon colors, cyberpunk, anime, cartoon, gaming cliches, or excessive ornamentation. Every design should feel timeless, premium, exclusive, and suitable for an operating system used by professionals.
